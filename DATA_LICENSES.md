# Data sources and terms

- Synthea sample data (MITRE): synthetic patients, free for research and education. https://synthetichealth.github.io/synthea/
- RxNorm API (US National Library of Medicine): used under NLM's terms of service. https://lhncbc.nlm.nih.gov/RxNav/
- openFDA drug labels (US FDA): public data, not validated for clinical use. https://open.fda.gov/terms/- DDInter 2.0 (Computational Biology & Drug Design Group): CC BY-NC-SA 4.0. data/processed/candidate_pairs.csv and the ddinter_level column in data/interaction_rules.csv are derived from it and shared under the same licence. https://ddinter2.scbdd.com/terms/
